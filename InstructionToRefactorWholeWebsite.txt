Web design is the combination of visual design, usability, content, and technology. A good webpage should be:

Clear: Visitors immediately understand its purpose.
Consistent: Colors, spacing, buttons, and typography follow a common style.
Responsive: It works well on phones, tablets, and desktops.
Accessible: Text is readable, controls are usable, and images have descriptions.
Fast: Avoid unnecessary images, animations, and complex code.
